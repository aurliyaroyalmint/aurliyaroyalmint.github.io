Questions, answered

Good to know.

Everything you may want to know before Darood Live arrives on Google Play.

What is Darood Live?+

Darood Live is a faith-centered companion for reading Darood-e-Sharif, counting Tasbeeh, following personal progress, reading duas and viewing selected Ziyarat collections.

Which languages are available?+

The app is designed around Arabic, Hindi, Hinglish and English. You can select three preferred languages for Darood captions and Duas.

Does the app save my reading progress?+

Yes. When you submit a Tasbeeh count, Darood Live can preserve it in your daily, weekly and monthly reading history so you can revisit your progress.

Is Darood Live available on Google Play?+

The Android release is being prepared. The official Google Play link will be added here once the app is approved and live.

Is Darood Live a substitute for religious guidance?+

No. The app supports personal reading and reflection. For religious rulings or individual guidance, please consult a qualified scholar you trust.
